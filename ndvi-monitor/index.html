How This Works

What is NDVI?

Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) measures plant health from satellite imagery. It compares near-infrared (reflected by healthy plants) and red light (absorbed by plants). Values range from -1 to 1:

  • Green > 0.6 — Dense, healthy vegetation
  • Yellow 0.3–0.6 — Moderate or sparse vegetation
  • Red < 0.3 — Bare soil, water, or stressed crops

How to use this app

  1. Sign in — Click "Sign in with Google" to authenticate with Earth Engine.
  2. Drag the slider — Move the time slider at the bottom to see NDVI change month by month.
  3. Click anywhere on the map to see a detailed NDVI trend chart and stress alerts.
  4. Draw a field — Use the polygon/rectangle tools to outline a rice paddy, then name and save it.
  5. Check the dashboard — Click ☰ to see your saved fields with live health status.
  6. Compare months — Toggle "Compare" to view two months side-by-side.
  7. Export — Click "Export PNG" in the trend chart panel to download the chart.

Data source

Satellite imagery from Sentinel-2 (ESA), processed by Google Earth Engine. Area of interest: rice-growing region near Battambang, Cambodia.
